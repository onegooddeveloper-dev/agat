Privacy Policy — Agat: encryptor

Effective date: November 1, 2026

1. Data we collect

None. The app does not collect, store on servers, or share with the developer or third parties any personal data, messages, contacts, device information or usage data. There are no ads, analytics or trackers.

2. Data stored on your device

Encryption keys, your contact list (names you choose), settings and, if you enable it, conversation history are stored only on your device in encrypted form. Uninstalling the app deletes this data.

3. Clipboard

When you return to the app, it checks the clipboard to offer decrypting a message. The clipboard content is not saved or sent anywhere. You can turn this off in Settings.

4. Message delivery

The app does not send messages itself. You send the encrypted text through a messenger or email app of your choice; that service's privacy policy applies to the delivery.

5. Purchases and free trial

The full version is purchased through Google Play; payment data is processed by Google under its Privacy Policy. The developer receives from Google only the order details needed to account for the sale.

To keep the free trial from resetting on reinstall, the app stores two dates — the trial start and the latest time the app was opened — in Google Block Store on your device and, if backup is enabled, in your Google account backup. The developer has no access to this data.

6. Children

The app is intended for users aged 18 and over.

7. Changes

Changes will be announced by updating this page with a new effective date.
